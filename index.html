📋 Hardware Commissioning Protocol & Pre-Flight QA
8 MANDATORY VALIDATIONS

📈 Step-by-Step Commissioning Workflow

Mandatory sequential electrical and firmware validation gates preventing component damage during initial power-up.

Pre-Flight Quality Assurance Sign-Off

0% Complete (0/8 Tests Passed)
GATE 01
Visual & Star-Ground Cold Continuity Test
Power completely DISCONNECTED. Multimeter in continuity mode. Verify 0.00Ω between all subsystem ground pins and star GND perfboard node. Confirm no solder bridges across 5V and GND.
GATE 02
Unloaded LM2596 Output Trimmer Adjustment
Connect 18650 battery bank. Flip rocker switch ON with ESP32 removed. Measure LM2596 output voltage. Carefully turn multiturn potentiometer until DMM reads exactly 5.00V ±0.03V DC.
GATE 03
Transient Load & Brownout Protection Verification
Connect 1000µF capacitor to 5V rail. Apply a 1.5A transient dummy load. Monitor 5V rail on oscilloscope or DMM min-hold. Confirm voltage dips no lower than 4.75V during motor startup pulse.
GATE 04
Master ESP32 Firmware Flash & Serial Telemetry
Plug USB-C cable into ESP32 DevKit. Upload cloudrover_master.ino at 921600 baud. Verify serial monitor output displays CPU frequency 240MHz, FreeRTOS queue created, and heap > 210KB.
GATE 05
I2C Bus Addressing & VL53L1X Calibration
Run I2C scanner routine on GPIO21/GPIO22. Confirm device acknowledged at 0x29 (VL53L1X). Place target at 100mm, 500mm, and 1000mm. Confirm readings accurate to ±2mm.
GATE 06
ST7735 SPI Display & Eye Animation Framerate
Initialize TFT at 27MHz SPI clock. Trigger idle animation loop. Verify smooth 45 FPS eye blinking without visual tearing, artifact glitches, or back-buffer corruption.
GATE 07
Dual H-Bridge Motor Rotation & Emergency Halt
Drive tracks forward at 50% PWM duty cycle. Verify both tracks spin in correct forward orientation. Place hand 120mm in front of faceplate. Confirm VL53L1X triggers immediate motor brake within 20ms.
GATE 08
SIM7600E-H Cellular Network Registration & GPS
Send AT+CSQ (Signal strength > 15). Confirm AT+CREG? returns registered (0,1 or 0,5). Transmit test HTTP POST payload to cloud server. Confirm server ACK received within 1500ms.
